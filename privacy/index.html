Privacy at Playmatic

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This page is a draft and will be reviewed before ads are switched on.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. If you choose to make one (players 13 and older only), we keep your sign-in email address and your saved progress, nothing else.
  • Without an account, your game progress is saved on your own device, in your browser, and is not sent to us. With an account, a copy is also kept in your account so it is never lost and follows you to other devices.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Today the site shows no ads. When ads are switched on, they will come from Google, and this page will say so plainly (see "Ads" below).

What is saved on your device

Playmatic and its games use your browser's storage (called "local storage") to remember:

  • your progress in each game (levels, stars, coins, items you have unlocked);
  • your tickets and coin balances in the Big Top;
  • your sound and music settings;
  • whether you have already seen the opening;
  • the answer to the age question, if you were asked it (see below).

Without an account, this stays in your browser on your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser's site data for this site erases it, and it does not follow you to another device or browser.

Accounts (optional)

Players who are 13 or older can make a Playmatic account by signing in with Google or with a link we email to them (and, later, with Apple). An account is never needed to play.

  • What we keep: the email address and account id that Google or Apple gives us when you sign in, the player name you choose (please make one up: never your real name; names are checked for rude words), and your saved progress: your tickets and coin balances, your settings, your sticker book, each game's progress, the answer to the age question, the color of your peg person, and one older copy of each save as a backup. We do not receive or keep your name, photo, contacts or password.
  • Why: only to keep your progress safe and let you play on more than one device. It is never used for ads and never sold or shared.
  • Where: accounts are run by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), our service provider, and stored in the United States. See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Under 13: players who tell us they are under 13 cannot make an account; their progress stays on their own device.
  • Deleting it: open Settings → Your account → Delete my account. Your account and every save in it are removed from our database right away. Your device keeps its own copy and keeps playing without an account. You can also ask us to delete it (see Contact, and how to delete your account).

What we do not collect

We run no analytics or tracking of our own. We do not collect names, phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location. We keep an email address only if you choose to make an account (see Accounts). The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like every web host, receives your IP address and basic browser details when it delivers the pages, and keeps server logs under its own privacy statement.

The age question

Before an account or the Big Top's ad booths are offered to a player, we ask one question: the year you were born. We ask only for the year. The answer is saved on your device, and with your account if you make one. Players under 13, and players who choose not to answer, can play every game; accounts are not offered to them, the ad stations stay closed for them, and no sign-in or advertising code is loaded.

Ads

Today: there are no ads on this site and no advertising code is loaded.

When ads are switched on, this is how they will work:

  • Ads appear only inside the Big Top, and only when a player chooses to watch one in exchange for tickets. Tickets can be traded for coins in our games; they have no cash value.
  • Ads will be provided by Google (Google AdSense, "H5 Games Ads"). Google and its partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can switch off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings or at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for their choices about cookies and personalized ads before any ad is shown.
  • No advertising is shown, and no advertising code is loaded, for players who told us they are under 13 or who did not answer the age question.

Children

Playmatic is made for a general audience. Accounts are offered only to players who tell us they are 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • To erase everything Playmatic has saved, clear the site data for this site in your browser's settings.
  • You can play every game without ever visiting the Big Top.
  • If you made an account, you can delete it at any time: Settings → Your account → Delete my account.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Important changes will be noted here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to ask us to delete your account and its data: shoppyspreelive@gmail.com
