Privacy at Playmatic

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. If you choose to make one (players 13 and older only), we keep your sign-in email address, the player name you choose and your saved progress.
  • Without an account, your game progress is saved on your own device, in your browser, and is not sent to us. With an account, a copy is also kept in your account so it is never lost and follows you to other devices.
  • Friends and playing together in the town are only for account holders who are 18 or older. Friends see your player name, your peg person's color and where you are in the town while you are online. There is no chat.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Today the site shows no ads. When ads are switched on, they will come from Google, and this page will say so plainly (see "Ads" below).

Who runs Playmatic

Playmatic is made and run by an independent game developer in the United States. "We" on this page means whoever runs Playmatic. To reach us, see Contact at the end of this page.

What is saved on your device

Playmatic and its games use your browser's storage (called "local storage") to remember:

  • your progress in each game (levels, stars, coins, items you have unlocked);
  • your tickets and coin balances in the Big Top;
  • your sound and music settings;
  • whether you have already seen the opening;
  • the answer to the age question, if you were asked it (see below).

Without an account, this stays in your browser on your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser's site data for this site erases it, and it does not follow you to another device or browser.

Accounts (optional)

Players who are 13 or older can make a Playmatic account by signing in with Google or with a link we email to them (and, later, with Apple). An account is never needed to play.

  • What we keep: the email address and account id that Google or Apple gives us when you sign in, the player name you choose (please make one up: never your real name), and your saved progress: your tickets and coin balances, your settings, your sticker book, each game's progress, the answer to the age question, the color of your peg person, and one older copy of each save as a backup. We do not receive or keep your name, photo, contacts or password.
  • Why: only to keep your progress safe and let you play on more than one device. It is never used for ads and never sold or shared.
  • Where: accounts are run by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and, for friends, the Firebase Realtime Database), our service provider, and stored in the United States. See Firebase's privacy and security information.
  • Under 13: players who tell us they are under 13 cannot make an account; their progress stays on their own device.
  • Deleting it: open Settings → Your account → Delete my account. Your account, every save in it, your player name and everything of yours in friends (below) are removed from our databases right away. Your device keeps its own copy and keeps playing without an account. You can also ask us to delete it (see Contact, and how to delete your account).

Friends and playing together

Friends let people you know see you in the town, walk around it with you and play the town's toys together. They are for account holders only, and only for players who are 18 or older by their answer to the age question. To join, you also type the friends password we give to testers and tick "I'm 18 or older".

  • What is stored: your friend code; your friends list and friend requests (a request is kept for 14 days at most); your player name and peg color as friends see them; while you are online, where you are (in the town, the part of the town and the spot you are standing on, or which game you are playing, and which swing or seat you are on); the last few things you did in the shared town, such as poking a toy (a kind of action, which toy, and when); and, in the Thumb Hug Tent, where your thumb is on your screen while it is touching it. It is stored in the Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Who sees it: only your friends see your player name, your peg person and where it is in the town, what you do there, and your thumb in the Thumb Hug Tent. A player you send a request to sees your player name and peg color so they can answer it. Nobody can list players or search for them; a friend is added only with their friend code, and only when both of you say yes.
  • No chat: there is no free-text chat. Friends see only player names and things done in the town; nothing anyone types is shown to anyone else.
  • Your sign-in email: when you join friends, the email address you sign in with is kept so the person who runs Playmatic can see who each tester is. Only that one account can read it, and the join screen says so. No other player can see it.
  • Removing friends: on the Friends page, Remove ends a friendship for both of you, Mute hides a friend's pings, and Switch friends off takes you out of the town for your friends at once. Delete my account removes all of it: your friends list on both sides, your requests, your code, your player name and color, where you were, and your sign-in email. A request you sent to someone else stays with them until they answer it or its 14 days run out; it holds only their own friend code and a time.
  • Party games: games played together online are not on the site yet. Before they open, this page will say what they keep.

Testers

The person who runs Playmatic can make a friend a tester, which lets them try unfinished games, or let them into another private menu. Each of these lists keeps the friend's account id and player name, and only that one account can read it. A person is taken off a list when it is switched off for them, or when they delete their account.

What we do not collect

We run no analytics or tracking of our own. We do not collect phone numbers, contacts, photos or precise location. We keep an email address only if you choose to make an account (see Accounts). The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like every web host, receives your IP address and basic browser details when it delivers the pages, and keeps server logs under its own privacy statement. The pages load nothing from any other company except Google Firebase, for accounts and friends.

The age question

Before an account, friends or the Big Top's ad booths are offered to a player, we ask one question: the year you were born. We ask only for the year. The answer is saved on your device, and with your account if you make one. We count carefully: someone counts as 13 (or 18) only once the whole year of that birthday has passed. Players under 13, and players who choose not to answer, can play every game; accounts are not offered to them, the ad stations stay closed for them, and no sign-in or advertising code is loaded. Friends are offered only to players who are 18 or older by their answer.

Ads

Today: there are no ads on this site and no advertising code is loaded.

When ads are switched on, this is how they will work:

  • Ads appear only inside the Big Top, and only when a player chooses to watch one in exchange for tickets. Tickets can be traded for coins in our games; they have no cash value.
  • Ads will be provided by Google (Google AdSense, "H5 Games Ads"). Google and its partners may use cookies and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can switch off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings or at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland will be asked for their choices about cookies and personalized ads before any ad is shown.
  • No advertising is shown, and no advertising code is loaded, for players who told us they are under 13 or who did not answer the age question.

Children

Playmatic is made for a general audience. Accounts are offered only to players who tell us they are 13 or older, and friends only to players who tell us they are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • To erase everything Playmatic has saved on your device, clear the site data for this site in your browser's settings.
  • You can play every game without an account, without friends and without ever visiting the Big Top.
  • If you joined friends, you can remove any friend or switch friends off at any time on the Friends page.
  • If you made an account, you can delete it, with everything in it and in friends, at any time: Settings → Your account → Delete my account.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Important changes will be noted here before they take effect.
