Delete your Playmatic account

A Playmatic account is optional. You can delete yours at any time, and you can keep playing without one.

In Playmatic

  1. Open Playmatic and sign in, if you are not signed in already.
  2. Tap the speaker button at the top to open Settings.
  3. Tap Your account, then Delete my account, and confirm.

Your account and every save in it (your sign-in email address, your player name, your tickets and coins, your sticker book, each game's progress and their backups) are removed from our database right away. The device you are on keeps its own copy of your progress and keeps playing without an account.

By email

If you cannot sign in, write to shoppyspreelive@gmail.com from the email address of the account and ask us to delete it. We will delete the account and everything in it, and write back when it is done.
