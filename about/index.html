About Playmatic

Playmatic is a small family of games that all happen in one place: a kids' playmat, the kind of printed town rug with roads, buildings and trees that children push toy cars across. Every game is played with toys on that rug.

The games

  • Switchyard. Toy trains roll out of tunnels. Flip the junction levers so each train reaches the station of its own color before the round ends.
  • Cookie Defense. An ant colony marches along the rug's roads toward a giant cookie. Set out toys to defend it, and merge them into better ones.
  • Toy Coaster Park. Start with an almost empty mat and build a theme park on it: paths, rides, stalls and a roller coaster laid piece by piece, for little peg-people guests.
  • Topple Trail. A chain reaction built from toys on the rug: dominoes, marbles, ramps and a wind-up car. Put the missing pieces back so that one flick sets off the whole chain.
  • Rug Rally. A toy car races along a road printed on the rug. Tap to turn at every bend, collect hubcaps, and see how far you get before the car tumbles onto the carpet.

The games are still growing: new levels and toys arrive often.

How it works

Open the playmat, tap a place in the town, and play. There is nothing to install and no account is needed: your progress is saved in your own browser. If you like, players 13 and older can make a free account so their progress follows them to another phone or computer. You can add Playmatic to your phone's home screen to open it full screen like an app.

The town also has a Big Top, a circus tent. When it opens, players who choose to can watch a short ad there to earn tickets, and trade the tickets for coins in any of the games. It is always a choice, tickets and coins are only for use inside the games, and they can never be turned into money.

What we care about

  • Games that are fun in the first minute and fair for as long as you play.
  • No gambling, no loot boxes, no paid chance, and nothing that tricks you into tapping.
  • Everything on screen drawn and every sound made by the game itself, so it loads fast, even on an old phone.
